What is AIG?

AIG is a hackathon intersecting with the International Collegiate Artificial Intelligence Contest (ICAIC), inviting local students to develop innovative startup ideas that leverage AI for positive impact.

Come with an idea, or develop one along the way. Refine it through AI workshops, seminars and mentorship, then pitch it at the AIG Semi-Finals, with selected teams progressing to the Finals.

Who can join?

Pre-University Track

Students studying in Singapore at JCs, Polytechnics or ITEs.

University Track

Students studying in Singapore at local universities.

Team size

3 to 5 students.

Join with an existing startup idea, an early-stage concept, or form a team and develop one together.

Why join?

  • Develop and pitch your startup idea
  • Learn practical AI tools and applications
  • Gain exposure through AIG × ICAIC activities
  • Opportunity to join the Beijing AI & Innovation Exposure Trip

What will you experience?

  • AI seminars and hands-on workshops
  • Mentorship for your startup ideas
  • Networking with ICAIC participants, students, founders and AI professionals from Singapore and overseas
  • Industry and ecosystem visits
  • Pitch at the AIG Semi-Finals and Finals
  • Optional: AI Innovation Ecosystem Exposure Trip to Beijing (6D5N, February 2027)

  3. 7 Dec 2026, MonAIG × ICAIC Activities (optional). Selected ICAIC talks, sessions and networking activities. Optional for AIG teams, with bonus points for participation.

  6. 9 Jan 2027, SatAIG Finals. The top 10 teams present their improved solutions and compete for the final awards.
